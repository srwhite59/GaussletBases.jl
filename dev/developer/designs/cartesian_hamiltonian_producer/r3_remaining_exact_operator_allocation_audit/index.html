R3 Remaining Exact-Operator Allocation Audit

Status: completed historical measurement. HP-R3REM-AUDIT-01 grants no production source authority.

Disposition

After terminal G-G workspace reuse, this audit separated the remaining Cr2 q4 exact augmented-operator allocation. Pass 088A identified terminal unit-nuclear U_GG factor lookup and Gaussian-sum construction as the largest in-wrapper owner. Neutral raw blocks, terminal kinetic/moment products, exact augmented transforms, route setup, and audit replay were measured separately.

That result led to the independent HP-R3UN-* authority and its implemented source. Current numerical contracts are:

  • terminal G-G products;
  • unit-nuclear Gaussian sums;
  • same-construction base reuse.

This page no longer defines a future implementation lane.

Historical Evidence

Manager-log Passes 088 and 088A preserve the measurement protocol, exact bucket timings and allocations, ignored probe boundary, interpretation, and the source-lane recommendation. Passes 089-089B record the later unit-nuclear implementation and validation.

The durable conclusion is ownership, not the old machine-specific timing values:

  • terminal G-G product buffers were no longer the dominant remainder;
  • unit-nuclear U_GG was a distinct terminal numerical owner;
  • route/setup and audit replay costs were not part of that kernel;
  • attribution evidence did not authorize source changes by itself.

Boundaries

HP-R3REM-AUDIT-01 remains measurement history only. It does not authorize:

  • source, test, tool, or driver edits;
  • extension of terminal product or unit-nuclear kernels;
  • route/raw-block setup cleanup;
  • residual, MWG, IDA, Qiu-White, or terminal-basis changes;
  • persistent caches, workspaces, metadata, reports, artifacts, or public APIs;
  • Cr2 facade, artifact, solver, or production workflow claims.

Any new optimization must use current subsystem authority rather than reviving this completed audit.
