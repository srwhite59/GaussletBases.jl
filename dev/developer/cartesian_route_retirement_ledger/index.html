Cartesian Route Retirement Ledger

This is a compact cleanup index, not source authority or a future architecture plan. Machine authority and exact deletion contracts live under the Cartesian Hamiltonian producer design index. Git history preserves the earlier development-era classifications and measured oracle details.

Current Route Boundary

The live producer follows:

parent and common shellification
-> terminal lowering and retained contracts
-> PQS or White-Lindsey terminal realization
-> exact one-body and IDA assembly
-> optional residual/supplement composition

The former replacement story ending in CartesianUnitPairs, CartesianPairOperatorPlans, and broad CartesianPairBlockMaterialization is abandoned. It is not the destination for current route work.

Pair-Ladder Exception

The pair-ladder retirement preserves exactly:

src/cartesian_pair_block_materialization/pqs_source_axis_transforms.jl
pqs_source_axis_transform_facts_from_pgdg_axes

That helper is called by the current route to construct mapped-COMX axis facts. Its existing path, output, and fingerprints remain governed by the mapped-COMX contract. The reduced module owns no pair inventory, pair plan, source-safe-term pilot, source-shell bridge, readiness object, or global materialization path.

Rules For Future Cleanup

  1. Prove committed callers, exports, load-time effects, serialization, and artifact ownership before requesting deletion.
  2. Prefer deleting an orphaned surface and its test pressure over introducing an adapter.
  3. Preserve active numerical kernels even when an abandoned wrapper once used them.
  4. Validate current endpoints, not the vocabulary of the retired route.
  5. Treat ignored probes and old reports as historical evidence, not compatibility obligations.
  6. Require a new docs-only amendment before restoring any retired API.

Preserved Numerical Owners

Current CPB geometry, parent axes/factors, shellification, lowering, retained units, transform contracts, terminal PQS/White-Lindsey realization, mapped COMX, raw Gaussian blocks, exact one-body, IDA/MWG, residual Gaussians, PRFs, represented Hartree, artifacts, and drivers remain independently owned. A retirement record does not broaden authority over these surfaces.

Older ledger rows mentioning MOVE_TO_CPB_PROVIDER, adapter/readiness ladders, pair inventories, or future global pair assembly are superseded historical planning. Consult the commit that contained them only when archaeology is explicitly required.
